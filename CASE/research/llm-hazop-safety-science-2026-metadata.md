## Why It Matters

This source is valuable for AI-HYSYS-Skill because HYSYS work often feeds review-stage process safety, HAZOP, safeguard, and operating-envelope discussions.

The paper is not HYSYS runtime evidence. Its value is process-safety boundary evidence:

- LLMs can generate HAZOP-style worksheets from engineering inputs in research settings.
- Similarity to an expert worksheet does not equal final safety acceptance.
- Validity of scenarios and diversity of safeguards must be checked, not assumed.
- Human expert review remains the final acceptance layer.

## Boundary For This Repository

Use this source to reinforce that AI-HYSYS-Skill may prepare HAZOP support material from an existing validated HYSYS case, exported KPIs, PFD/P&ID inputs, and operating-envelope data.

Do not use it to claim autonomous HAZOP approval or process-safety signoff. Any HAZOP, safeguard, interlock, SIS, or operating-envelope recommendation must remain advisory until reviewed by qualified humans under the project safety procedure.
