Real-Time Quality Control: How HPCL Uses Industrial AI to Improve Refining Processes

HPCL’s Mumbai Refinery faced a major issue with product quality giveaways, as assessing product quality was both challenging and time-consuming.

The refining team used Aspen AI Model Builder™ and Aspen HYSYS® to build and deploy AI-based soft sensors that could predict the desired product quality parameters in real-time enabling HPCL to:

  • Minimize quality giveaways and maximize yield, saving an estimated $100,000–$400,000 USD per year
  • Reduce the need for frequent sampling and reliance on lab analysis, cutting associated costs by around 15–20%
  • Gain real-time visibility to product stream quality
