## Why this source matters

This official release introduces AspenTech AVA as an industrial AI platform for operations. The useful evidence for AI-HYSYS-Skill is not that AVA directly controls Aspen HYSYS. The useful evidence is that Emerson/AspenTech describes industrial AI as domain-aware, operations-embedded, data-contextualized, and supported by first-principles models and large language models.

For this repository, the source supports the current boundary:

1. AI should assist workflows around existing validated models, data context, recommendations, and reporting.
2. First-principles models remain part of the trusted engineering context.
3. AI-assisted recommendations should remain inside an auditable operations workflow.
4. The open-source skill must not claim it can reproduce AVA or replace commercial operational AI platforms.
